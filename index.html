🎡 How to Play Roots Resort

1. Earn gold

Answer Greek and Latin root questions in the Root Challenge. Correct answers earn gold, and consecutive correct answers increase your reward up to 100 gold per question.

2. Build your resort

Open the Resort Shop, choose Rides, Vendors, or Paths, then click Buy & place. Click a spot on the grass to build. Dirt paths cost 5 gold per tile and can be placed repeatedly.

3. Look after your visitors

Visitors get hungry, thirsty, tired, bored, and need the bathroom. Build food stands, lemonade stands, bathrooms, and rides to help them. A carousel reduces boredom by 40 and exhaustion by 25; a Drop Tower reduces boredom by 50.

4. Help visitors find their way

Visitors prefer paths but can walk on grass to reach attractions. They queue for rides and services. Click a visitor to see their needs, or drag them to a ride or vendor.

5. Grow your resort

You begin with 10 visitors. Every 100 gold you spend on construction adds another visitor. Click an existing building to sell it for half its original price. Click the entrance to choose a different gate style.

6. Control your game

Use Pause to stop time, Speed to run the resort faster, and Save to store progress in this browser.
