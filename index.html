🎡 How to Play Roots Resort

1. Earn gold

Answer Greek and Latin root questions in the Root Challenge. Correct answers earn gold. Build a streak of correct answers to earn bigger rewards, up to 100 gold per question.

2. Build your resort

Browse the Resort Shop to choose what you want to add. Select Buy & place, then click an open space in your resort to build. You can also add paths to help visitors get around.

3. Take care of visitors

Visitors have five needs: boredom, hunger, thirst, exhaustion, and bladder. Watch their needs and build places where they can have fun, get what they need, and rest. Visitors may leave if too many needs get too high.

4. Help visitors get around

Visitors prefer paths but can also cross grass to reach destinations. They may wait in line when a place is busy. Click a visitor to see their name and needs, or drag them toward a destination. Drag an empty patch of grass to move the camera and explore more land. Drag decorations directly to rearrange them for free.

5. Grow and customize

You begin with 10 visitors, and more arrive as you spend gold on construction. Click a building to see its options, including moving or selling it. Explore the resort to discover other ways to customize it.

6. Save your progress

Use Pause and Speed to control time. Save stores progress in this browser. Use Get Save Code to copy your resort into a code you can keep, and Load Save Code to restore it later.
